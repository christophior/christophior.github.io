Christophior LLC · Austin, Texas Independent software company · Est. [[2023]] Letter no. 1 · November 2026

We build products, run them, and keep the ones that earn their place.

To whoever is reading this,

Christophior is an independent software company. It owns and operates a small set of consumer products: a pregnancy companion for iPhone, a television app for people who bring their own channels, and a guide to what is opening next in Texas. Each has its own name and its own site. Each is designed, built, shipped, and supported here.

We are small on purpose. A product that one careful operator can run well beats a product that needs a department, and we would rather run three things properly than ten things loosely. Nothing here has outside capital, and nothing here is for sale.

Below is the portfolio as it stands today, the rules we operate by, and a record of what has changed. It will be updated as things ship.

How we operate

Four rules
1.

Own the whole thing

Design, build, ship, support. No handoffs, so no excuses.

2.

Build what we would use

Every product started as something we wanted and could not find. That filter has saved more time than any framework.

3.

Run it for years

Launch is the start. Release notes, support, and the boring parts are the product.

4.

Tools where they earn their place

AI for the mechanical parts. Hands on everything else.
