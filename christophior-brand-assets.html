Rules

Short enough to remember.

  • Two colorsPaper #FAF8F3 and black #000000. Reversed: ink #0D0D0C and cream #F5F2EA. Grey #6B675F for secondary text only.
  • One typefaceInstrument Sans, regular and medium. Tight tracking on anything large.
  • The markA dot, then the word. The dot is the height of the lowercase letters and never changes color independently of the text.
  • SpaceWhen in doubt, make the mark smaller and the margin larger. At least 80 percent of any header stays empty.
  • The big wordmarkCropped at the baseline, edge to edge. Used only on the site footer and the link preview.
