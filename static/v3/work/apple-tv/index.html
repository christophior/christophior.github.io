What it does

[[Add a playlist by URL or file, get a guide that is readable from the couch, and press play.]]

Playlists[[M3U and M3U8 by URL or file. Multiple providers side by side.]]
Guide[[XMLTV EPG with now and next, built for the Siri Remote rather than a mouse.]]
Playback[[HLS through AVKit with the native tvOS player, so scrubbing, audio tracks, and subtitles behave like every other app on the box.]]
Favorites[[Pin channels. Recently watched on the top shelf.]]
Not doing[[No bundled content, no accounts, no recording. It plays what you bring.]]

How it's built

[[Swift and SwiftUI on tvOS, AVKit for playback, and a small on-device store so the guide loads instantly after the first fetch.]]
