How I work

The best things I have made started as something I wanted and could not buy.

Build for myself firstIf I would not use it every week, I do not build it. That filter has saved me more time than any framework.01
Ship small, run it longThe smallest version that solves the problem well, then months of operating it. Support, updates, and the boring parts are the product.02
AI where it earns its placeI use it for the parts that are mechanical and keep my hands on the parts that are not. Nowhere else.03
